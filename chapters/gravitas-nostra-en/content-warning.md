# ⚠️ Content Warning

This novel contains content that may be distressing, including:

▸ **Mental Health**: Depression, self-harm, and suicidal ideation or attempts.

▸ **Abuse & Violence**: Bullying, physical assault, and sexual harassment.

▸ **Discrimination**: Homophobia, as well as prejudice based on race, religion, or social class.

▸ **Language**: The use of colloquialisms and profanity for the sake of realism.

**📌 Author’s Intent**:

This work is **not intended to romanticize violence or death**. Rather, it aims to shed light on hidden realities and serve as a voice for those who have been so deeply oppressed that they have been left without the opportunity to speak for themselves.

**💡 Additional Notes**:

▸ Not recommended for readers under the age of 18.

▸ For adult readers who may believe that depression is merely _overthinking_, or that same-sex love is an _abnormality_—please set these beliefs aside before you begin. Silence and a lack of acceptance are precisely what push many individuals—both in this story and in real life—to the very edge.

▸ If this content affects your mental well-being, please stop reading and prioritize your own self-care.

▸ If you feel overwhelmed, please know that you do not have to go through this alone; we encourage you to reach out to a mental health crisis hotline in your country.

Please exercise discretion while reading.
