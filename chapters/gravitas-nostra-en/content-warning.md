# ⚠️ Content Warning

This novel contains content that may cause emotional distress (Trigger Warning) including:

▸ **Mental Health**: depression, self-harm, suicide

▸ **Abuse & Violence**: bullying, physical assault, sexual harassment (non-consensual sexual harassment)

▸ **Discrimination**: discrimination based on gender, race, religion, and classism.

▸ **Language**: use of colloquial language and profanity for authenticity.

**📌 Author’s intention**:

This work is not intended to romanticize violence or death, but to reflect the hidden truth, to echo the voice of those who are oppressed, to give a voice to those who don’t have a chance to speak.

**💡 Additional tips**:

▸ Youth under 18 years of age and adults with views from different eras (those older than the Millennial generation) should be closely advised.

▸ If these contents affect your state of mind **please stop reading and take care of yourself first**.

Please use your discretion when reading.
